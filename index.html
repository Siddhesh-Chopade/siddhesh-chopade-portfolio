🛡️ Sentinel — Mobile Threat Detector

Aug 2025 – Jan 2026 · Group Project (3 members)

Worked with two teammates to build a full-stack dashboard for detecting and monitoring malicious mobile apps based on their declared permissions. Users submit an app's name, package, and permission list; the backend computes a weighted risk score, classifies the app as benign, suspicious, or malicious, and stores the result in MySQL. Suspicious or malicious results automatically generate a contextual alert with a short explanation and a recommended action, and the dashboard surfaces the fleet-wide picture through charts, a searchable table, and a live threat ticker.

Project TypeSecurity analytics dashboard
Core FocusPermission-based risk scoring
Key FeatureContextual alerts & threat ticker
Key contributions

Built as a three-person team: I designed the weighted permission-based risk-scoring engine and the normalized MySQL schema, one teammate built the Node.js/Express REST API around it, and the third built the HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript dashboard frontend; we integrated all three layers together.

  • Designed the weighted permission-based risk-scoring engine that normalizes results to a 0–100 scale and classifies apps as benign, suspicious, or malicious.
  • Structured the normalized MySQL schema for apps, permissions, app-permission mappings, and alerts.
  • Coordinated with teammates on REST endpoints for app scanning, permission catalog lookup, alerts, and aggregate stats.
  • Defined the contextual-alert logic (short explanation + recommended action) consumed by the dashboard.
  • Collaborated on the risk-distribution and top-permission charts, searchable fleet table, and live threat ticker.
How it works
1User scans an app by entering its name, package, and declared permissions.
2Backend sums weighted permission risk and normalizes it to a 0–100 score.
3Flagged apps trigger a contextual alert and appear on the dashboard's charts and live ticker.
